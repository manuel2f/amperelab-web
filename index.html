Smarter Than Android

Why other apps give misleading numbers

The OS reports «Charging» as long as 5 volts are present, even when battery is dropping. AmpereLab breaks down four distinct physical states:

State Threshold What it really means
● Charging > 0.5 W entering battery Power is actively filling your cell. The animated radial gauge reflects instant flow.
● Charging Paused Connected, ~0 W net Adaptive charging, thermal throttle, or 80% battery protection limit active. Excluded from session average so night tests stay comparable.
● Losing Charge Negative net power while plugged in Your phone is consuming more power than the charger or cable can supply. Common in GPS car mounts or faulty cables.
● Battery Full 100% capacity reached Trickle maintenance mode. The battery is completely topped off.

Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Does AmpereLab require root or ADB permissions?
No. AmpereLab reads directly from the standard Android BatteryManager and device sensor counters without requiring root or computer setup via ADB.
Does it consume battery while measuring?
Measuring power takes minimal CPU cycles. When screen is on, the radial gauge renders smoothly at 60 fps. When your screen is turned off during a test, the background foreground service samples periodically with minimal overhead.
Why does the wattage fluctuate while charging?
Modern smartphones follow charging curves (such as USB-PD PPS). A phone that accepts 30W when empty will taper down to 15W, 8W, and 2W as it approaches 100% or heats up. AmpereLab displays this real-time behavior accurately.
Is my data private?
Yes, 100%. The application doesn't even declare the Android INTERNET permission. No analytics, no crash beacons, no cloud accounts. All your tests stay exclusively on your phone.
